Eight federal agencies drawn as constellations. The points of light around each one are its positions, one point per 250 jobs. The bright stars are its long-serving staff, people with fifteen or more years of service, and a star's brightness is their experience. Together they hold the agency's institutional memory. A position can be refilled in a month. A long-serving star relights only if that person comes back.

  • This is a sketch, not a dataset. The figures are rough approximations from published reporting (GAO, Partnership for Public Service, Pew, agency announcements, and for ACF, tallies by former staff). Before you show it, check each one against OPM's workforce data and edit the EVENTS block in the source.
  • Two layers, two measures. The points are positions: one per 250 jobs, simply filled or empty, so a hire relights one at once. This is what headcount reporting counts. The constellation stars are long-serving staff: each agency's people with 15 or more years of service, 12 stars per agency, so one star is about 8% of its long-serving staff. A star's brightness is its experience. Together they measure institutional memory. Only reinstating the same people relights a star; new hires don't, because no one hired today has fifteen years of service.
  • The lines between stars connect the long-serving staff. A line is drawn while both of its stars are present.
  • The share of long-serving staff is an estimate for each agency (from 20% at TSA to 40% at IRS, FAA and NIOSH); check OPM length-of-service data. Long-serving staff feel each kind of cut differently: probationary firings remove none of them; deferred resignations and early retirements remove them at 1.6 times the agency's overall rate; RIFs remove them at the overall rate. Losses smaller than half a star don't put one out.
  • Reinstated staff return at full brightness (the October 2025 RIFs, reversed in November; NIOSH reinstatements in January 2026). New hires, such as the IRS seasonal hires, return faint.
  • The ACF Social Services Block Grant team is the small separate triangle. Everyone with that expertise left, so it stays dark.
  • Shutdowns: while an agency is unfunded, its stars and people go dark grey and stop twinkling, because nothing is moving and no one is being paid. A day counter runs under its name. Furloughed and unpaid staff are still employees, so no experience is lost and the stars return as they were when funding comes back. The exception is people who quit, like the more than 1,100 TSA officers who left during the 76-day DHS shutdown in 2026. Two shutdowns are shown: Oct 1 – Nov 12, 2025 (government-wide, 43 days) and Feb 14 – Apr 30, 2026 (DHS only). A four-day partial lapse from Jan 31 to Feb 3, 2026 is mentioned in a caption but not drawn.
  • The timeline ends at the present, October 2026. It shows no forecast. Funding is extended through Dec 11, 2026, so no third shutdown is drawn.
  • How changes show: when an event happens, the caption appears first. Then the stars of each agency it affects change one at a time, and a count of the people lost or returned ticks up under its name. A star that goes out flares and leaves an amber afterglow. A returning star arrives with a closing ring. New hires shift from blue to gold as they gain experience, and send out a small ring at every new year.
  • The meters: "Positions filled" counts the points. "Institutional memory" adds up the long-serving staff's years of experience against September 2024. The percentages under each name use the same two measures.
  • Memory: press and hold "Remember Sep 2024" to see the sky as it was before the cuts. When you let go, amber rings briefly mark the experience that has not come back. Gallery mode does this on its own at the end of every loop.
  • Installation mode is for a room with a knob: nothing moves on its own. Turning the knob moves through time in either direction, and holding the space bar (or the knob's push button) remembers Sep 2024. The knob can send arrow keys, + and −, or scroll-wheel movement. After three minutes untouched it fades back to Sep 2024 for the next visitor. Press I to enter, or add #install to the address.
  • Planetarium mode is for projection on a ceiling: no text while the sky plays, then the names and meaning are revealed at the end. Add #planetarium to the address to open straight into it.
  • Gallery mode plays the full timeline on a loop, pausing on each event. Add #gallery to the end of the address to open straight into it.
